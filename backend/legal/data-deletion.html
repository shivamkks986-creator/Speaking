Data Deletion Request

SpeakMate AI · Last updated: February 2026

You may request permanent deletion of your SpeakMate AI account and all associated data (practice sessions, chat history, resume uploads, subscription records except those legally required for tax purposes) using any of the methods below.

Option A – Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open SpeakMate AI on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete My Data.
  3. Confirm with your password or Google re-authentication.
  4. Your account is queued for deletion immediately and permanently removed within 30 days.

Option B – Email us

Send an email to speakmate.support@gmail.com from the address you registered with, using the subject line:

Delete my SpeakMate AI account

Include your registered email in the body. We will confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Profile: email, display name, photo, learning goals.
  • Practice content: voice recordings, transcripts, chat history, resume PDFs.
  • Progress data: streaks, XP, badges, quota counters, usage stats.
  • Push notification tokens.

What we retain (legal minimum)

  • Purchase records (order IDs, dates, amounts) — retained for 7 years per Indian GST tax law.
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics that cannot be linked back to you.

Play Store subscriptions

Deleting your app data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. To cancel:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select SpeakMate AI → Cancel subscription.

Questions?

Contact us any time at speakmate.support@gmail.com.
