Privacy Policy

SpeakMate AI · Last updated: February 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SpeakMate AI ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email, display name, and profile photo when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Practice content: your voice recordings, transcripts, resume uploads, and chat messages you submit for AI feedback.
  • Usage data: feature usage counts, session length, device model, OS version, crash logs, and coarse language settings.
  • Purchase data: Google Play purchase tokens (used solely to verify your subscription). We never see your card details.
  • Advertising identifiers: Android Advertising ID for ad personalization by Google AdMob (opt-out available in device settings).

2. How We Use Information

  • To power AI features (tutor chat, interview mock, resume review, pronunciation scoring).
  • To verify Premium subscriptions and grant or revoke access.
  • To serve and measure ads for free-tier users via Google AdMob.
  • To send optional push notifications (streak reminders, learning tips) which you can disable in device settings.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and improve model quality (aggregated, non-identifying analytics).

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third parties. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (authentication + crash reporting) – policy
  • Google Play Billing (subscription purchases) – policy
  • Google AdMob (advertising for free-tier users) – policy
  • Google Gemini & Anthropic Claude (AI model inference) – content is transmitted only for the duration of the AI response and is not used for model training.

4. Data Retention

Account and practice data are retained while your account is active. If you request deletion (see Section 6) we permanently remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is legally required (e.g., transaction records for tax law).

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us so we can delete it.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time:

  • In-app: Settings → Account → Delete My Data.
  • Web: Data Deletion Request Page.
  • Email: speakmate.support@gmail.com

7. Security

All traffic uses TLS 1.2+. Purchase tokens and access tokens are stored server-side with encryption at rest. We restrict production database access to authorised engineers only.

8. International Transfers

Data is processed on Google Cloud servers primarily located in Asia and the US. By using the App you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when features change. Material changes will be announced in-app. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.

10. Contact
